## Memos

**P01 Checkout stress monitor.** Do not proceed. Checkout stress monitor is classified as prohibited. Infers emotions of people at work or in education (except for medical or safety reasons): prohibited by Art 5(1)(f) since 2 Feb 2025. Annex III 1c: Emotion recognition. 8 obligations, starting with: Do not place on the market or use; redesign or stop (Art 5(1)(f)); Use according to the instructions for use (Art 26(1)); Human oversight by competent, trained, empowered staff (Art 26(2)). Next deadline: 02 Dec 2027.

**P02 Consumer loan scoring.** Proceed only with high-risk controls. Consumer loan scoring is classified as high-risk. Annex III 5b: Creditworthiness or credit score of natural persons (except fraud detection). 20 obligations, starting with: Risk management system across the lifecycle (Art 9); Data governance: relevant, representative, bias examined (Art 10); Technical documentation (Annex IV) (Art 11). Next deadline: 02 Dec 2027.

**P03 Card fraud alerts.** Proceed. Card fraud alerts is classified as minimal. No prohibited practice, no Annex I or III ground and no transparency trigger found: minimal risk, voluntary codes only.

**P04 CV shortlisting assistant.** Proceed only with high-risk controls. CV shortlisting assistant is classified as high-risk. Annex III 4a: Employment: recruitment and selection (job ads targeting, screening, evaluating candidates). 12 obligations, starting with: Risk management system across the lifecycle (Art 9); Data governance: relevant, representative, bias examined (Art 10); Technical documentation (Annex IV) (Art 11). Next deadline: 02 Dec 2027.

**P05 Warehouse shift planner.** Proceed only with high-risk controls. Warehouse shift planner is classified as high-risk. Annex III 4b: Employment: decisions on terms, promotion, termination, task allocation from behaviour or traits, or performance monitoring. Derogation claimed (Art 6(3)(d) preparatory task), but the system profiles people, so Art 6(3) keeps it high-risk. 7 obligations, starting with: Use according to the instructions for use (Art 26(1)); Human oversight by competent, trained, empowered staff (Art 26(2)); Input data relevant and representative, where controlled (Art 26(4)). Next deadline: 02 Dec 2027.

**P06 Online exam proctoring.** Proceed only with high-risk controls. Online exam proctoring is classified as high-risk. **P07 Resident reliability score.** Do not proceed. Resident reliability score is classified as prohibited. Social scoring that leads to detrimental or unjustified treatment: prohibited by Art 5(1)(c) since 2 Feb 2025. 1 obligations, starting with: Do not place on the market or use; redesign or stop (Art 5(1)(c)).

**P08 Life insurance pricing.** Proceed only with high-risk controls. Life insurance pricing is classified as high-risk. Annex III 5c: Risk assessment and pricing in life and health insurance. 20 obligations, starting with: Risk management system across the lifecycle (Art 9); Data governance: relevant, representative, bias examined (Art 10); Technical documentation (Annex IV) (Art 11). Next deadline: 02 Dec 2027.

**P09 Customer service chatbot.** Proceed, with transparency duties. Customer service chatbot is classified as transparency. Interacts directly with people: they must be told it is an AI (Art 50(1)). 1 obligations, starting with: Tell people they are interacting with an AI, at first contact (Art 50(1)).

**P10 Ad creative generator.** Proceed, with transparency duties. Ad creative generator is classified as transparency. Generates synthetic content: outputs must be marked as AI-generated, machine-readably (Art 50(2)). 1 obligations, starting with: Mark generated content as AI-made, machine-readably (Art 50(2)).

**P11 Stock forecasting.** Proceed. Stock forecasting is classified as minimal. No prohibited practice, no Annex I or III ground and no transparency trigger found: minimal risk, voluntary codes only.

**P12 Face search database.** Do not proceed. Face search database is classified as prohibited. Builds facial recognition databases by untargeted scraping of images from the internet or CCTV: prohibited by Art 5(1)(e) since 2 Feb 2025. 1 obligations, starting with: Do not place on the market or use; redesign or stop (Art 5(1)(e)).

**P13 Emergency call triage.** Proceed only with high-risk controls. Emergency call triage is classified as high-risk.
